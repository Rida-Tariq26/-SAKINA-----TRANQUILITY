Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about Sakina's capabilities, privacy, and boundaries.

How does Sakina balance spiritual reflection and cognitive science?

Sakina views spiritual grounding and cognitive science as complementary disciplines. It uses classical Islamic psychology (Ilm al-Nafs) to understand spiritual meaning and purpose, while applying cognitive neuroscience (CBT, ACT, nervous system regulation) to provide structured, practical exercises for daily stress.

Is Sakina free to use?

Yes, completely free. Sakina is a free AI mental wellness companion offering full access to conversational emotional support, Islamic mindfulness and Dhikr practices, AI mood tracking, and reflective journaling without subscription fees or paywalls. You can also optionally connect your own Gemini API key (BYOK).

Can I use Sakina if I am not religious?

Absolutely. While informed by classical Islamic psychology, Sakina offers multiple interaction modes, including a dedicated scientific mode focused purely on cognitive reframing, somatic breathwork, and emotional regulation without spiritual citations.

Is Sakina a replacement for a therapist or doctor?

No. Sakina is strictly an educational and supportive wellness companion for daily reflection and stress management. It does not provide medical diagnoses, psychiatric therapy, or emergency care. In acute distress, built-in crisis safeguards immediately surface professional emergency helplines.
